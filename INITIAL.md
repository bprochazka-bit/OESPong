Initial Prompt
== PONG ==

* When started, show a screen saying: "Owen Entertainment System Presents"
* The text should fade away and show the title: "PONG" in a large and bright colors while playing music
* When the start button is pressed, show player selection below the PONG title image.
* Player chooses 1 or 2 players and presses "Start"
* The screen fades away and 2 "paddles" show on either side of the screen.
* Paddles are thin, vertical rectangles
* Paddles are controlled using the up and down buttons from either controller.
* Player 1 is red and Player 2 is blue.
* The background is black.
* The scoreboard is south up
* When a player reaches 3 points, the game restart.
* The ball is a dot.  When the ball touches a paddle, the ball reflects, bouncing in the other direction.
* If the ball goes off the side of the screen without the paddle reflecting it, the opposing player earns a point and the ball starts again from the middle.
* The ball bounces off the top and bottom of the screen
* At the start of the game, player one has the ball on it's paddle, pressing the "A" button releases the ball toward the other side at an agngle relative to the direction the paddle was moving when the "A" button was pressed.
* If a paddle was moving when the ball is hit, the speed and direction of the paddle's movement will alter the angle of reflection.
